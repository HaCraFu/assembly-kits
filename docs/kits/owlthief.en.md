# Owl Thief

<img src="../../kibot/owlthief/render/owlThief-3D_top.png" width="200" height="200" alt="Owl Thief Front">
<img src="../../kibot/owlthief/render/owlThief-3D_bottom.png" width="200" height="200" alt="Owl Thief Back">

This circuit board is a recreation of the [Owl Thief by Timo Schindler](https://shop.blinkyparts.com/en/detail/43c51d7fe354486d92ab0097a1106e60) in SMD assembly.

This simple circuit ensures that a low voltage (e.g., an almost empty battery) is boosted and makes two LEDs light up.

The circuit is called a [Joule Thief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joule_thief), hence the name.
