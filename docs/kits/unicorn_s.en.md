This circuit board is practically identical to the [Rainbow Unicorn](https://shop.blinkyparts.com/en/detail/2be126ff15944653973e8ab5393be2b1).
Only a different switch was used and the labeling was slightly modified.

This beautiful colorful assembly kit comes from [Binary Kitchen e.V.](https://www.binary-kitchen.de).

It is very easy to solder: Only 4 colorful LEDs, the battery holder and the switch.
